Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production desktop application icon for LiteSim 3D, a lightweight ROS 2 3D robotics and LiDAR simulator, used in macOS Dock and Linux app launchers.
Primary request: Design ONE polished, memorable, clean application icon. A dark cobalt-blue rounded-square tile contains a large sculptural isometric cube and a single LiDAR scanning orbit, communicating a lightweight simulated 3D world.
Subject: One simple ice-white and pale-cyan isometric cube centered prominently, composed of three broad crisp surfaces, subtly beveled, strong dimensional silhouette. One bright turquoise elliptical scanning arc wraps horizontally around the cube: its rear part passes behind the cube, its front part is visible, with exactly three well-spaced substantial cyan point returns as the arc finishes. The cube remains the dominant shape. The scan ring should feel like a precise sensor sweep rather than a planet or atoms.
Style/medium: premium restrained 3D-rendered desktop app icon, minimal geometric construction, satin surfaces, gentle studio highlights, sharp clean contours, subtle depth, quiet professional engineering character.
Composition/framing: square 1024 x 1024 canvas. Single centered upright rounded-square tile, front-facing, no perspective rotation of the tile. Tile occupies about 82 percent of canvas width and height, balanced transparent margins on every side, no cropping. The mark fills most of the tile with ample breathing room. Optimize bold recognizability at 32 px, 48 px, and 64 px.
Color palette: deep cobalt-blue tile with a very restrained lighter upper-left surface gradient, luminous ice-white cube, cyan scan arc. Strong contrast; no rainbow.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent alpha outside the rounded-square tile, including all four corners; the tile itself is opaque.
Text: none. No letters, no numbers.
Constraints: ONE icon only, no alternate versions, no icon grid, no app window mockup, no desktop scene, no captions, no watermark, no logo text, no robot face, no landscape, no ground grid, no fine particle cloud, no intricate wireframes, no stars, no excessive glow, no outer rectangle background. Keep the icon visually balanced and its silhouette extremely clear.
